# Soft-Shell Crab Toast

## Ingredients

- 2 cups packed Italian parsley leaves
- 2 jalapeño peppers, seeded and coarsely chopped
- 2 tsp fresh lemon juice, more as needed
- 1/4 tsp salt, more as needed
- 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil, more as needed
- 6 (1/2-inch-thick) slices country-style bread or pain de mie
- 1 large garlic clove, halved and peeled
- Unsalted butter, as needed (optional)
- 6 (3 oz) wild jumbo soft-shelled crabs, cleaned
- Black pepper, as needed
- Lemon wedges, for serving

## Directions

In a blender, combine parsley, jalapeño, lemon juice and salt. Add oil and 3 tbsp water. Purée until sauce is smooth, adding additional water if needed, a tbsp at a time, until you have a thick yet pourable sauce. Transfer sauce to a bowl and add more salt or lemon juice, or both, if needed.

Heat broiler. Place bread slices in a single layer on a baking sheet. Broil, turning once, until golden brown, 1-2 minutes per side. Rub toast with cut garlic. Drizzle with oil or slather with butter (butter is nice on pain de mie), remove from pan and set aside.

Lightly coat crabs with oil and season with salt and pepper. Transfer, belly side up, to a large baking sheet (you can use the same one you used for the toast). Broil, turning once, until crisp and golden, about 2 minutes per side.

Place one crab on top of each toast. Spoon a generous amount of sauce over each crab and serve with lemon wedges on the side.
