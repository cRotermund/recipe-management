# NY Cheesecake

## Ingredients

- **Crust**
- 3 cups graham cracker crumbs
- 1/2 cups melted butter

- **Filling**
- 5 packages cream cheese
- 1 1/4 cups granulated sugar
- 3 tbsp all purpose flower
- 1 1/2 tsp lemon zest
- 1 1/2 tsp orange zest
- 5 eggs
- 2 egg yolks
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1/4 cup heavy whipping cream

## Directions

**Crust**
In a large bowl, combine graham cracker crumbs and butter press into bottom and edges of 9" spring-form pan, keeping layer relatively thin, and freeze. 

**Filling**
In large mixer bowl, beat cream cheese and sugar on medium speed for 3 minutes.

Mix in flour, lemon and orange zest.

Add eggs and egg yoks, one at a time, beating after each addition.

Mix in vanilla and cream.

Pour batter over frozen crust.  Back for 10 minutes at 500 degrees F, then reduce heat to 200 degrees F and bake for an additional 60 minutes.  Cool on a rack for 2 hours.

Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 6 hours.
