# Red Cabbage, Carrot and Broccoli Stem Latkes with Caraway and Sesame

![Photo of Red Cabbage, Carrot and Broccoli Stem Latkes with Caraway and Sesame](attachments/32C45F4A-E717-4A02-96BB-83D1E7A2AD99.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 5 cups shredded red cabbage
- 1/2 lb carrots, shredded (about 1 1/2 cups)
- 1 1/2 cups shredded peeled broccoli stems
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds
- 2 tsp caraway seeds
- 1 tsp baking powder
- Salt to taste
- 3 tbsp oat bran
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 3 tbsp cornmeal
- 2 tbsp buckwheat flour
- 3 eggs, beaten
- About 1/4 cup canola, grape seed or rice bran oil

## Directions

Heat the oven to 300°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment and place a rack over another sheet pan.

In a large bowl mix together the shredded cabbage, carrots, broccoli stems, baking powder, sesame seeds, caraway seeds, salt, oat bran, flour, cornmeal and buckwheat flour. Taste and adjust salt. Add the eggs and stir together. Let the mixture sit for 10-15 minutes.

Begin heating a large heavy skillet over medium heat. Take a 1/4 cup measuring cup and fill with 3 tbsp of the mixture. Reverse onto the parchment-lined baking sheet. Repeat with the remaining latke mix. You should have enough to make about 30 latkes.

Add the oil to the pan and heat for 3 minutes or until hot. When it is hot (hold your hand a few inches above – you should feel the heat), slide a spatula under one portion of the latke mixture and transfer it to the pan. Press down with the spatula to flatten. Repeat with more mounds. In my 10-inch pan I can cook four at a time without crowding; my 12-inch pan will accommodate 4 or 5. Cook on one side until golden brown, about 4-5 minutes. Slide the spatula underneath and flip the latkes over. Cook on the other side until golden brown, another 2-3 minutes. Transfer to the rack set over a baking sheet and place in the oven to keep warm.

Serve hot topped with low-fat sour cream, Greek yogurt or crème fraîche.
