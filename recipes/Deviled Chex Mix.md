# Deviled Chex Mix

![Photo of Deviled Chex Mix](attachments/9B4B8535-C981-43F9-B1A2-A4A415230920.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 3 Tablespoons sugar
- 1 Tablespoon paprika
- 1 Teaspoon ground chili powder
- 1 teaspoon curry powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/3 cup canola oil
- 1 cup assorted unsalted nuts
- 3 cups Rice Chex
- 3 cups Corn Chex
- 3 cups Wheat Chex
- 1 cup small cheese crackers
- 1 cup Salty Stix pretzels
- 1 cup Wheat Thins OR 1 cup Pita chips:

## Directions

1 whole wheat pita, split, spray with olive oil spray on cut sides, sprinkle lightly with Kosher salt, cut into 1″ squares, and bake at 250 degrees F for 15 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes. Set aside to cool. Leave the oven on…

In a small bowl, mix together the seasonings and set aside.

Pour the oil in a large roasting pan, and warm it in the oven. Stir in the nuts and roast for a few minutes until fragrant. Add the cereals, pretzels, crackers and chips and stir well until evenly coated. Sprinkle with the seasonings and stir until evenly coated. Bake for 30-60 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes. Spread on paper towels to cool. Store in an airtight container.
