# Best Ever Greek Yogurt Chocolate Chip Muffins

![Photo of Best Ever Greek Yogurt Chocolate Chip Muffins](attachments/102B1EA9-537A-49F1-9088-1AEED620938F-989-000000E950FE3154.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups all purpose flour
- 1/2 cup white sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup skim milk
- 3/4 cup low fat Greek yogurt (1-2%)
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup dark or semi-sweet chocolate chips

## Directions

Preheat your oven to 350°F and line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners.

In a large bowl whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt.

Add the milk, Greek yogurt, vegetable oil, eggs and vanilla and stir everything together with a wooden spoon or a spatula.

Gently stir in the chocolate chips once everything is combined, just until they're distributed evenly throughout.

Portion the batter into the 12 muffin cups evenly and bake at 350°F for 22-25 minutes, or until the muffins are cooked through (test with a toothpick!) and are a light golden brown colour on top.

Let the muffins cool for several minutes and enjoy warm.
